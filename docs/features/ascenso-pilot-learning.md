# Ascenso pilot learning workflow

## Matching decisions

Candidate rows accept an optional selection reason and a required reason for recording a skip. A skip records evidence without creating or permanently excluding a pair. Selection and its reason commit together; activation remains separate. Reasons and server-computed scores/track/pair IDs appear in Operational events exports. Record concrete needs (availability, experience, existing relationship, stated preference), not sensitive personal speculation. Review recurring reasons before changing the algorithm.

## Manual work register

The director assigns one register owner and reviews entries weekly in an organization-approved restricted spreadsheet. Use these columns; no helpdesk platform is needed:

`Date | Cohort ID | Category | Related application/match/event ID | Owner | Minutes spent | Outcome | Follow-up due | Brief process issue`

Categories: support request, intake correction, matching review, rematch, email recovery, Calendar recovery, access/grant change, closeout, reporting/export. Record each intervention once; reference the existing event rather than copying application answers, email addresses or meeting notes. Include work outside Ascenso and distinguish active work time from elapsed waiting. Review totals per category and per cohort, recurring failure causes and unresolved follow-ups. Compare with a documented pre-pilot estimate using the same definitions; do not claim measured time savings from an unsupported recollection.

## Funnel export

Download **Pilot funnel (observed evidence)** from the existing report export menu. It provides one application/role row with stable participation ID, application/approval dates, first observed authenticated dashboard access, selection/activation evidence, first logged meeting date, distinct logged meeting dates, shared goals and named survey-response count.

- First observed access starts with the Phase 15 implementation. It is not necessarily the person's first login; historical access is unknown and not backfilled from account creation, approval or identity linkage. Blank means unknown/not observed, not proof the person never signed in.
- Selection comes from board approval timestamps or recorded selection events, including removed selection history. A proposed row alone is not a selection. Activation uses its own timestamp; unknown remains blank.
- Meeting dates come from logs, excluding future dates. Two distinct logged dates are evidence of repeated pair activity, not verified attendance or individual retention. Multiple entries on one date do not inflate that signal. Pair activity/goals appear on both participants' rows; deduplicate match records in the detailed exports when counting program totals.
- Application-role rows are the denominator. Report approved and enrolled denominators separately, disclose missing historical evidence, choose a cutoff/cadence with the director, and retain the definition version/export timestamp. Named survey counts describe responses, not satisfaction scores. Cross-program identity does not merge cohort results.
- First access capture failure does not deny an otherwise authorized dashboard; operators investigate the diagnostic and preserve unknown evidence rather than fabricate a timestamp.

## Early feedback

Use an organization-approved external survey after the first two to four weeks or first scheduled conversation. Built-in mid/end-year waves remain unchanged to preserve the released definition; no new survey builder is introduced. Ask whether participants reached their partner, scheduled/met, encountered friction, found the pairing useful, and need program help. Decide and disclose whether responses are named, optional contact details and who can read them. Do not describe a linked form as anonymous without verifying its actual settings.

The director assigns an owner to review early responses within an agreed period, contact participants who requested help and enter resulting operational work in the register. Record response denominator and nonresponse; do not wait until year-end to learn about inaccessible sign-in or failed introductions.
